Photomontage

I might have misunderstood the assignment and just did a photomontage for fun but I could argue that a message my photomontage gives off is the idea of self identity. Self identity is this important belief of who you are and it’s what makes you unique compared to other people. Your self identity is shaped by experiences you’ve encountered throughout your life and is further built by relationships, values, and interests. Fostering this idea of self identity creates self growth since as humans we can identify strengths and weaknesses that help us make the right decisions. Rather than being influenced by others, you are able to connect to your true self. This creates a healthy relationship with yourself and everyone around you. Everyone has different ideas and beliefs and that's because they have gone through different situations that have evolved them as a law of life to adapt to things. Having a strong sense of self identity can help you navigate through obstacles in life and help you achieve a sense of purpose.

In my photomontage, my idea was having this main picture and having lots of smaller pictures make up parts of the main picture based on colors so it blends in. I tried my best with the blending but I don’t think it’s really that good… My main picture was me holding a camera kind of symbolizing the smaller pictures being some pictures I took. Most of the images are ones I took myself or friends took of me with a few images I grabbed from online. They all represent all the things that make up who I am. I love to play badminton, eat lots of food, and play the violin. I own a cat and am a Chinese American living in New York. I used a lot of masking on the smaller images to change their gradient, brightness, and saturation to better match the bigger picture. I used the screen blending mode to create the effect of the camera flashing and tried to make the whole picture a bit blurry. My images I grabbed from just google searching them and converting into JPGS.
