Creative Statement:

I really like the way my eyes and mind process things. This sounds very self-centered but I’m just thankful to be able to see and explore things that are able to make me happy. I think this reflects a lot of my work in creative fields. I have some experience in various forms of digital art such as coding, music, animation, and photography. I feel a connection to all my work and it helps me express a lot of my emotions and thoughts. A lot of my creative aspect comes from what I consume going through social media and trends I see people following. Being able to engage in daily life and online life helps inspire me to make what I want to portray into reality. I don’t really have a preferred canvas or a focus at all but I enjoy creating things that feel right to me. Things that have a strong visual appeal or aesthetic is something that sparks a vision in my eye because it’s everywhere and can be shown in different ways. It allows viewers to see different perspectives that are welcomed when looking at art.
