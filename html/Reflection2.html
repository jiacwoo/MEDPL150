"With ‘AI slop’ distorting our reality, the world is sleepwalking into disaster" by Nesrine Malik

The evolution of AI has introduced a lot of things for humanity and I would like to 100% agree that the creation of it is a major turning point in our future. The basis of AI is this piece of technology that runs on data to simulate tasks that would normally require a human to do. Tasks that analyze data and make based decisions are just a small part of the potential AI holds. You can ask AI questions and it can break it down for you, helping you understand or even have it create a whole story for you based on what you ask. By giving more information to AI, we can personalize it to do things that you specifically ask for.

Recently the use of AI has been further applied in creative works because of social media. On social media, I see a lot of people generate art and content sometimes to promote products and apps like advertisements but also to spread misinformation. It’s because it’s so easy to make and produce with the help of AI that these contents are being brought into social media so easily. Sometimes it’s so realistic that you can’t figure it out until the end and because of the attention that it’s gathering, you have groups of people who believe in fake information. Malik basically says that you are put “deeper and deeper into subjective worlds rather than objective reality”. AI is trained to do what you tell it to do so by creating this piece of work tailored to what you want to show, is showing people this canvas that is subject to your belief. An example of this would be how everyone believes in the news because it’s a record of what is real and happening. By making AI create fake news recordings, people that don’t know it’s fake will be believing in something that's not real.

However despite all this, I don’t think AI is the problem but rather the people who use it. My generation, Gen Z, have been introduced to the technology of AI at a pretty young age and have been accustomed to using it in our daily lives. I use it to ask questions and to give insight on things I have never done. Sometimes I would take a really good picture but there is this one thing that ruins it like the lighting or even an object in the way. I could photoshop it myself or edit the lighting but it’s just so much easier asking AI to do it for me. You could argue that it’s fake that way and not authentic but likeeee nobody would know except you if it’s something that small(LOL).

I think the biggest fear for AI right now is that it might be able to replace humans one day. If all it requires is data to run it, then what is stopping AI from making its own and becoming self automated? AI has already taken over some jobs in the market and will continue to as long as people keep programming it to be better. There is a fine line between where AI can be destructive or constructive. I don’t know if in the future AI can take over the world but I do think that we are very far from that future.
