Graphic Novel: Motherhood in crisis

These graphic novels all portray the lives of African pregnant women that I didn’t know people can go through. Well as a guy, I can’t imagine giving birth much less in the conditions these women were in but anyone can see that these conditions were completely horrible. I couldn't believe it when I read Kadiatu’s story and saw that she didn’t even have 3 dollars to borrow for the taxi to save her son’s life. I was shocked because maybe this was back then like the 1900’s but this was only 6 years ago. 3 dollars is what I have to spend daily for 1 trip on the MTA. Not having an accessible doctor is very scary especially for a pregnant woman. Many women in Africa are experiencing this everyday and it’s crazy to think about. I mean maybe it’s because I live in New York where I see so many people, where even people my age are getting opportunities to branch out and be successful. But there are women out there in Africa who don’t even have the money to visit a nearby doctor. When my mom had me, the doctors had to do a c-section on me. I think I was too big or something? I forgot the reason but I was very healthy. I bring this up because it was really recent that I learned that c-sections aren’t normal. I actually genuinely thought that's how babies were born. Like it was normal for a doctor to cut open a woman’s stomach to bring the baby out. That alone sounded terrifying but unlike my mother, (I think) the women in these graphic novels didn’t have the choice. It was either because something was wrong or it was too late to give birth normally. I don’t even know how parts of the world can be that much technologically behind. I look at videos of people in China and they practically have flying cars. I was surprised when I saw the illustration of Memunatu’s village. People out in Africa are still living with straw roofs. This makes me very grateful for the life I am given. I had to search it up because the last time I heard of ebola was this youtube video I saw and that was like 10 years ago. I thought ebola was wiped out like we were fully cured of it. But according to google, it’s still very much active in parts of Africa, sub Saharan parts especially. The pandemic of Covid is the closest thing I can relate to and Covid was insane. I was in 8th grade and I saw adults arguing over toilet paper on my way to school. My ignorance on a lot of these topics makes me realize how much different my life is from these women simply because I wasn’t born in Africa. I feel bad for associating these negative events with Africa but I just didn’t know it was possible with how advanced the world is getting. I wish all women around the world the ability to be safe and happy.
